Squad Brawl

Real-time 3v3 mage arena. Equip your squad from the shared spell pool, study the enemy's loadout, then fight. You control one mage directly; the other two follow their loadouts on their own.

What this prototype tests: does the loadout (which mage gets which 4 spells, and countering the enemy's picks) matter when the fight itself is fast, skill-based action (dodging skillshots, positioning, timing)?
