Mage Squad: three 3D prototypes

Same core idea, three ways to play it: equip your team of mages with spells from a shared pool, look at the enemy's loadout, counter-pick, then fight. Every version uses the same 10 spells and the same three mage archetypes (Warden, Sorcerer, Adept), so you can compare the style of play rather than the content.

1 · Turn-based

Arena Tactics

XCOM / Into the Breach. Think, then act, with a chess-clock time bank.

  • 3 mages, 2 spells each
  • Move and act per mage on a 3D grid
  • Telegraphed Meteors, walls, line of sight
2 · Real-time action

Squad Brawl

Diablo / Battlerite. You control one mage, and two AI allies fight beside you.

  • 4 spells on Q W E R
  • Dodgeable skillshots
  • 3v3 arena
3 · Strategy (RTS)

Mage Commander

Age of Empires, lite. Design unit blueprints, run an economy, command an army.

  • Capture Mana Wells for income
  • Train units from your 3 blueprints
  • Destroy the enemy Sanctum

Needs an internet connection the first time (the 3D library loads from a CDN). If something breaks, a red box appears on screen: tell me what it says. Add ?quick to any URL to skip the loadout screen.
