Mage Commander

What this prototype tests: is it fun when your spell choices are an army composition? You design 3 mage blueprints before the battle, then play an Age-of-Empires-style match: capture Mana Wells for income, train mages from your blueprints, and command the army to destroy the enemy Sanctum. Your mages cast their spells on their own. Your job is macro (economy, what to train) and positioning.

Your blueprints

Each blueprint is an archetype plus up to 2 spells, and you can train as many mages from it as your mana allows. Click a blueprint to edit it, then click spells in the pool to equip or unequip them. Spells make a mage more expensive.

How a match works
  • Your Sanctum makes 4 mana/s. Each Mana Well you hold adds +1.5/s.
  • Stand next to a Well with no enemies nearby to capture it (5s, or 10s to steal).
  • Home turf: your mages regenerate within 16m of your Sanctum, and it shoots at attackers. Break the enemy army before you siege.
  • Up to 20 mages. At 15:00 both Sanctums start to crumble (sudden death).
  • You win by destroying the enemy Sanctum on the right.
