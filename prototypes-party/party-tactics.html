Form your parties

Every character is one player: pick a class, 3 skills and 5 passive points. Pairs of classes give party bonuses. Skills set each other up: stun, root, wet, burning, marked and exposed are setups that allies pay off for big combos. Each slot can be a human (hot-seat) or the AI.

Battlefield
Rules
Turn order. Each round both parties choose who acts 1st, 2nd and 3rd. The starting party acts first and last: A1 → B1 → A2 → B2 → B3 → A3. The start passes to the other party every round.
Downed. At 0 HP a character falls. An adjacent ally can Help Up (30% HP), or a Cleric can Resurrect from range. Bleeds out after 2 of its turns; any enemy hit finishes it.
