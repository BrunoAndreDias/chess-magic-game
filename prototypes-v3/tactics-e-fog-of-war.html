Prototype v3 · Variant E · Tactics with fog of war

Fog of War — scout, hide, bluff

Pick an archetype and two spells for each of your three mages. Then prepare the field in secret and deploy hidden. The enemy's loadout is secret until you see them cast.

What's different in this variant
  • 🌫 Fog of war on a bigger 12×12 map. You only see what your mages and Ward Stones see. Mages inside tall grass are invisible unless an enemy is next to them.
  • 🗺 Prepare the field before the fight. Spend 8 prep points on Ward Stones, Decoys, hidden Fire/Frost Runes and Tall Grass, then deploy your mages hidden. The enemy does the same.
  • ❓ The enemy loadout is secret. You learn a spell when you see it cast, or when you use the NEW Scry spell. NEW Veil turns an ally invisible.
  • ⏳ Longer battles. More HP and a bigger map. On round 30 the fog burns away (everyone is visible, healing is halved), so hiding can't stall forever. Cap: 60 rounds.
