How the battle flows

Act I · Preparation (rounds 1–4): damage spells are 🔒 sealed. Inscribe glyphs, raise walls, shield up, erase enemy glyphs.
Act II · Clash (round 5+): all spells unlock. Glyphs and rituals pay off.
Act III · Unraveling (round 30+): everyone takes growing damage each turn.

Glyphs (your mage must stand on your own glyph)

A mage can inscribe the elements of its equipped spells, on its own tile or an adjacent one. Max 6 per team: the oldest crumbles. Enemies erase a glyph by standing next to it and spending an action, or with a Meteor.

Prototype v3 · Variant F · Turn-based tactics with preparation

Arena Tactics: Rituals — equip your mages

Pick an archetype and two spells for each of your three mages. Your spells also decide which glyphs each mage can draw on the battlefield. The enemy's loadout is shown, so pick counters. Each mage gets one move and one action per turn, and both teams share a chess-clock time bank.

What's different in this variant
  • Act I · Preparation (rounds 1–4): damage spells are sealed. You race to inscribe element glyphs on the 12×12 board, raise walls and erase the enemy's glyphs.
  • Glyphs buff the mage standing on them (Fire +30% dmg, Frost root-immune, Storm +2 move, Earth shield, Life heal). Both sides can see every glyph.
  • 3 glyphs in a line or L → Grand Ritual: channel for 2 turns to unleash Inferno, Blizzard, Tempest, Bastion or Renewal. The enemy can break it with damage, a root, or by erasing a glyph.
  • Tougher mages (about 1.6× HP) → battles last about twice as long. From round 30 the arena unravels and forces a finish.
