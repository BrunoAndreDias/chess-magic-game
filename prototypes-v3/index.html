Arena Tactics: six variations

All six start from the Arena Tactics battle you liked and change one big thing. Every variation has longer battles than the original and a step for setting up before the fight.

Original Arena Tactics for comparison.

How the battle plays

A · More like chess

Chess Duel

Does it get better the more it feels like chess?

  • One mage acts per turn, alternating
  • 6 units with chess-like movement, and an Archmage "king"
  • Threat highlighting and an "Archmage in danger" warning, like check
B · Read the enemy

Foresight

Is it more strategic when you can see what the enemy will do?

  • Enemies show where they'll strike next turn
  • Dodge, wall off, interrupt, or Gust them into each other
  • Plays like a puzzle, with undo
C · Draft & combos

Elemental Draft

Deeper with a competitive draft, team mana and element reactions?

  • You and the opponent take turns picking spells, so each spell belongs to one side
  • Shared team mana: who gets to act?
  • Burning, Wet, Chilled tiles and their combos

Preparing the field

D · Build before the fight

Fortify

Is it satisfying to sculpt the battlefield, tower-defense style?

  • Build budget: walls, platforms, pylons, springs
  • Hidden rune traps
  • The enemy builds at the same time; both builds are revealed together
E · Information

Fog of War

Is preparation more interesting when it's about scouting and bluffing?

  • You only see what your mages and wards see
  • Hidden enemy loadouts, decoys and traps
  • Scry and Veil spells
F · Setup race

Rituals

Is it more fun when both sides prepare inside the battle and can disrupt each other?

  • Act I: 4 rounds with attack spells sealed; you inscribe glyphs
  • Act II: the fight
  • Grand Rituals that can be interrupted
