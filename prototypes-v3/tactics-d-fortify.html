Prototype v3 · Variant D · Preparation: Fortify

Fortify — equip your mages

Pick an archetype and two spells for each of your three mages. You can see the enemy's loadout, so pick counters. Next you prepare the field: fortify your half of the arena and deploy your mages. Then fight, turn by turn, with a chess-clock time bank.

What's different in this variant
  • NEW preparation phase: spend 12 build points in your half of a bigger 12×12 arena: stone ramparts, raised platforms, an Arcane Pylon turret, a Healing Spring, a Mana Font, and three kinds of hidden traps.
  • Deploy your 3 mages anywhere in your back 4 rows. Both sides' buildings are revealed at the same moment; traps stay hidden until someone steps on them.
  • NEW 👁 Reveal (every mage, key 4) exposes traps in a 3×3. Spells can smash ramparts and pylons; Meteor wrecks everything it lands on.
  • Tougher mages on a bigger field, so battles last about twice as long (~22–27 rounds against the bot).
