Prototype v3 · Arena Tactics · Variant B

Foresight — see their spells coming

Pick an archetype and two spells for each of your three mages, counter-picking the enemy you can see on the right. Then deploy, and fight a team whose every spell is announced one turn in advance.

What's different in this variant
  • Enemy intents (Into the Breach style): enemies declare their spells on tiles at the end of their turn — numbered red areas — and cast them, in that order, at the start of their next turn. You always act knowing exactly what's coming.
  • Answer the threat: step out, raise a Stone Wall in a bolt's path, Barrier, kill the caster, or disrupt it (Frost Nova / Ice Lance cancel its spell). Two NEW shove spells: 🌪️ Gust pushes a unit into pillars or into its allies' intents; 🪝 Tether pulls.
  • Prepare the field: a deployment step — place your mages and your 3 Ley Crystals (immobile, the enemy targets them; lose all 3 and you lose). The enemy deploys after you and reveals its first intents before round 1.
  • Longer battles (~2× the original, ~23 rounds): much more HP, plus ↶ Undo move for puzzling out each turn.
