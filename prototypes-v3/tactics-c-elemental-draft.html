Elemental Draft

What’s different in this variant
  • Draft instead of a free loadout: each spell exists once. You and the enemy pick in snake order (1-2-2-1…) until each has 6. Take what you need, or what they need.
  • Team mana instead of 1 action per mage: 3 mana on turn 1, +1 per turn up to 6, keep up to 2. Any mage may cast twice, so you choose who gets the mana.
  • Spells leave elements on tiles that react: 🔥 Burning, ❄ Chilled, 💧 Wet. Fire + Poison = 💥, Frost on Chilled/Wet = 🧊 Frozen, Lightning on Wet = ⚡ conducts. Two NEW combo spells: 🌧️ Rain, 🌀 Gust.
  • Prepare the field: after the draft you deploy your mages anywhere in your 3-row zone. Mages have about 2× HP, so battles last about twice as long.
