Prototype v3 · Arena Tactics variant A · “Chess Duel”

Chess Duel — equip your court

Six mages per side on an 8×8 board, moving like chess pieces. Your Archmage is the king: 3 spells, and if it falls you lose. The other five carry one spell each. You see the enemy's loadout, so pick counters — then deploy, then duel.

What's different in this variant
  • One piece per turn, like chess: activate a single mage (Move and/or Action, either order), then it's the opponent's turn. The chess clock ticks on every one of your turns.
  • Chess movement + a king: Archmage ♚ steps 1 any way · Warden ♜ 1–2 straight · Sorcerer ♝ 1–3 diagonal · Adept ♞ jumps in an L. Mages block line of sight, so screens and open lines matter. “Archmage in danger!” works like check.
  • Deployment step: after the loadout you place your six mages on your first two ranks. The enemy deploys in secret at the same time.
  • Longer battles (about 2× the decisions of the original): more HP, shorter ranges, cooldowns count in your turns. Sudden death from round 100.
