Siege Pact

A team castle siege prototype. One team breaks in, the other holds until dawn. You play one hero; bots fill the other seats and follow your team calls.

Side

Players per team

Your hero

Attackers win by destroying the Keep.
  • The ram only moves if someone holds E next to it. One pusher crawls, two walk, three roll. An Engineer counts as two.
  • Lanes are split by rocks. Switching lanes after committing costs real time, so call the lane early.
  • Dead attackers come back together, in waves every 10s.
Defenders win if the Keep stands for 5 minutes.
  • Boiling oil: each gate has two pads. It pours only while two defenders stand on both pads at the same time. It deals huge damage to the ram.
  • Engineers hold E at a gate to repair it or rebuild a broken one. A Barricade can plug a breach.
  • You see what your team sees. Scouting matters.
