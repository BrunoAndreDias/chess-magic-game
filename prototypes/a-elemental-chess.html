Elemental Chess Prototype A

What this prototype tests: do rock‑paper‑scissors types (Water › Fire › Earth › Water) plus a single one‑shot ability per piece turn a small chess‑like board into interesting decisions — under a chess clock? Each piece can either move or spend its once‑per‑game ability. Capture the enemy Monarch, or let their clock run out.

Type cycle

Arrow = beats. A piece can’t capture the type that beats it (Fire can’t take Water). Same types trade freely. Anyone can take a Monarch; the Monarch can take anything.

How to play
  • Turn: either move one piece, or spend one piece’s ability (each piece has one charge per game — the glowing dot).
  • Fire (F): slides diagonally up to 2. Ignite — destroy an enemy non‑Monarch within 2 squares (any direction, ignores blockers). Water is immune.
  • Water (W): slides straight up to 3. Tide — push every orthogonally adjacent enemy 1 square away; pushed off the board = destroyed (a Monarch just holds).
  • Earth (E): steps 1 in any direction. Raise Wall — put a permanent stone wall on an empty adjacent square.
  • Monarch (♛): steps 1 in any direction, captures anything. Royal Swap — trade places with any friendly piece.
  • Win: take the enemy Monarch, or the opponent’s clock hits 0, or the opponent has no legal action. 150 turns = draw.
  • Clock: only the side to move burns time; you gain the increment after each turn you complete.
