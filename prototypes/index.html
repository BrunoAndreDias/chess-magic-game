Magic Chess: three prototypes

Three throwaway prototypes, each testing a different idea of where the magic lives. All three are turn-based with no luck, use a Fischer chess clock (a time bank plus a bonus per move) and have a leader you must protect. Play a few games of each, then compare.

A · Magic in the pieces

Elemental Chess

Do type match-ups and one-shot abilities create interesting decisions?

  • Water > Fire > Earth > Water
  • Every piece has one ability charge per game
  • Walls permanently reshape the board
B · Magic in the clock

Chronomancer

Is it fun when your clock is both thinking time and mana?

  • Spells cost seconds from your own clock
  • Rewind, Haste, Freeze, Blink, Siphon
  • 6×6 mini-chess
C · Magic in the geometry

Sigils

Is it fun when spells emerge from the shapes your pieces form?

  • Line of 3 fires a beam
  • 2×2 square summons a new Adept
  • Protect your Oracle
